# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are the staff of Indian private, multi-branch K-12 school groups — teachers (PRT/TGT/PGT), guest/visiting faculty paid per lecture, and non-teaching staff (admin, transport, hostel, Class-IV). They are classroom-bound and largely desk-less, so the employee experience is phone-first even though the web app ships first. The web surface additionally serves HR operators, payroll accountants, principals, and group administrators who run people, attendance, leave, compliant payroll, hiring, and documents across campuses. When a screen must speak to more than one of these, the teacher/staff self-service view is primary and the HR/admin console is secondary.

## Product Purpose

Naveya (*nava* "new" + *nāv* "vessel / to navigate") is a compliance-grade HR & payroll platform built specifically for Indian educational institutions, delivered as multi-tenant SaaS with a single-tenant on-premise option. It exists because the incumbents (Keka, Zoho People, greytHR, HROne) are corporate-office tools that schools are forced to adapt to. Naveya matches them on India statutory payroll (PF/ESI/PT/TDS/gratuity/bonus/LWF) and leads on the things structural to schools: academic-calendar leave, vacation-staff vs non-vacation-staff pay logic, and multi-scale payroll that runs salaried staff and per-lecture honoraria in one statutorily-correct run. Built by Voxturr Labs in collaboration with ShriConnect, the technology arm of The Shri Ram Schools group. Success is a school running its whole staff HR — hire to F&F — on one audited system, with a single-click, variance-checked payroll run.

## Positioning

The only HRMS whose calendar, staff model, and payroll are shaped by how a school actually operates — not a corporate tool bent to fit one.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, rooted. The voice is plain and exact, never salesy or cute — it is handling people's pay, leave, and documents, and every screen should lower anxiety rather than perform delight. Rooted in Indian education without kitsch: Hindi and English are equals, the ledger/register is the cultural touchstone, and craft (Linear/Stripe-grade restraint) carries the premium feel rather than ornament.

## Anti-references

Not a generic SaaS dashboard: no indigo/blue-600 primary, no identical rounded-card grids for dense data, no KPI hero-metric row of gradient-number tiles, no glassmorphism, no side-stripe callouts, no uppercase tracked eyebrows as decoration. Not the incumbents' cluttered legacy-payroll look (greytHR/HROne density-without-hierarchy). Not decorative Indian kitsch (tricolour, saffron, paisley, rangoli, diya, mandala, temple arches, fake-Devanagari-look Latin). Not a cartoon assistant mascot (the Zia/Bella/Lisa lineage). Not the retired internal "Greenboard & Chalk" forest-green + marigold + serif direction. Money is never gradient'd, glowing, or truncated.

## Design Principles

- **The register is the product.** The ruled staff × period muster that resolves rightward into net pay is the hero surface on every screen — attendance, leave, payroll, documents — not a card dashboard. Structure (hairline rules, row rhythm, one accent) does the work.
- **Figures are sacred.** Every rupee and count is tabular, decimal-aligned, full-Decimal-precision, never truncated. Trust is earned through legible numbers.
- **Bilingual by construction.** Hindi/Devanagari is a first-class, weight-matched equal to Latin, wired (dual line-heights, no tight clamps), not bolted on.
- **Calm through restraint.** One brand chroma on chrome; the full state palette (success/warning/danger/info) is reserved for data meaning. Never encode state by hue alone — this is a money product for a wide audience.
- **Phone-first for a desk-less workforce.** The teacher/staff experience is designed for a phone in a corridor between classes; the web console serves the back office.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA across light and dark themes (verified contrast: body ≥4.5:1, large ≥3:1, placeholders held to body contrast). State is never conveyed by color alone (marks/icons/signs accompany every P/A/L/½ and every credit/debit) — red-green color-vision deficiency is common in the audience. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for every animation. Hindi + English throughout, with Devanagari rendered at its own roomier line metrics. PII (PAN/Aadhaar/UAN/ESIC/bank/salary) is field-level gated and never surfaced casually.
