Numerals are the brand. Every rupee figure is JetBrains Mono with tnum and lnum on, right-aligned, in the Indian lakh–crore grouping (₹1,26,44,180 — never 12,644,180). Nothing is abbreviated to 1.26Cr in a register: an administrator signing a record needs the whole figure. Anek Latin and Anek Devanagari share a skeleton, so the bilingual pairing sits on one baseline without optical mismatch.
